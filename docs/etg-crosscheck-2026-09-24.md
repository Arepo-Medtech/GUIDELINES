# eTG cross-check of the licensed-source doses (2026-09-24)

Every dose claim from a licensed source (AMH) in 61 guidelines was compared with **Therapeutic Guidelines (eTG)**, through the owner's subscription, on 2026-09-24 (owner decision). eTG figures are paraphrased; no eTG text is stored.

**621 doses checked: 246 match · 156 differ · 219 not covered by eTG. 299 eTG therapies the pages lacked were added** as licensed-source claims and sit in `docs/attestation-queue.md` as outstanding.

Each guideline page has an **eTG cross-check** section with the same detail. The original claims are unchanged, and the owner's acceptance on them stands. The rows below are the ones to look at first when checking doses against the subscription.
