# Unwell children with established diabetes in hospital

**Edition:** 1.1 · 2026-09-24 · **Status:** drafted and source-anchored; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** hospital assessment, ketone-guided insulin adjustment, hypoglycaemia rescue (glucagon) and pump troubleshooting for children with established type 1 diabetes who present unwell. ## Key points

- ⚠️ **Test blood ketones in every unwell child with established diabetes** — ketones can be raised even with normal glucose (e.g. starvation ketosis in gastroenteritis). [S1]
- ⚠️ **Never omit long-acting (basal) insulin**, even when unwell, fasting or hypoglycaemic. [S1]
- ⚠️ **In a child with known diabetes, regard vomiting as signalling insulin deficiency/hyperglycaemia.** [S1]
- **Interpreting BGL and ketones:** hypoglycaemia ≤3.9 mmol/L · normal pre-meal 4–7 and post-meal 4–10, ketones <0.6 · high >11, ketones ≥0.6 · very high >15 (check ketones), with ketones >1 meaning insulin is required. [S1]

## Why it happens and assessment

- **Insulin needs rise** with acute illness, after surgery and on corticosteroids; **they fall** with limited oral intake or increased fluid losses (e.g. gastroenteritis). [S1]
- **Hyperglycaemia, fever, glycosuria and ketonuria all add to fluid losses** and the risk of dehydration. [S1]
- **Examine conscious state (AVPU/GCS) and breathing** — a raised respiratory rate or work of breathing can point to DKA — plus hydration and the intercurrent illness. [S1]

## Investigate and exclude DKA

- **Investigations:** BGL; point-of-care blood ketones (urine ketones only where blood ketones cannot be done); **venous blood gas when BGL or ketones are raised.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Manage as DKA: BGL >11 (hyperglycaemic) together with pH <7.3 or bicarbonate <18 mmol/L.** [S1]

## Severe and rescue hypoglycaemia — glucagon

- ⚠️ **Confused, unconscious or seizing: IM glucagon.** Reconstitute 1 mg with 1 mL water; **0.5 mg (half the vial) if under 25 kg; 1 mg (the whole vial, 1 mL) if over 25 kg.** [S1]
- **Subcutaneous minidose glucagon** is for BGL under 4 mmol/L when oral treatment has failed or cannot be tolerated, **but without CNS impairment**; it avoids the nausea and vomiting of larger doses. [S1]
- **Minidose SC glucagon (1 mg in 1 mL; 1 insulin-syringe unit = 10 microg):** under 2 years **20 microg** (2-unit mark) · 2–15 years **10 microg for each year of age** (1 unit/year) · over 15 years **150 microg** (15-unit mark). [S1]

## Insulin adjustment — low or normal glucose

- **Low/normal BGL may need lower insulin doses, but insulin must not be omitted.** High ketones with low BGL is **starvation ketosis** and needs extra carbohydrate/glucose. [S1]
- **By blood ketone, for BGL <5 / 5–10 mmol/L:** <0.6 — reduce TDD by 20% / usual bolus (2-hourly checks) · 0.6–0.9 — reduce TDD by 15% / usual bolus · 1–1.4 — reduce TDD by 10% / usual bolus · 1.5–2.9 — **start IV fluids containing glucose; keep insulin going** / add 5% of TDD to the bolus (1–2-hourly) · ≥3 — **IV glucose fluids and continue insulin, which may need reducing** / add 5% of TDD. BGL under 4: hypoglycaemia pathway. [S1]

## Insulin adjustment — hyperglycaemia with ketosis, normal pH

- **Hyperglycaemia and ketosis with normal pH need extra subcutaneous insulin to clear ketones.** pH under 7.3: DKA pathway. [S1]
- **Correction bolus by blood ketone, for BGL 10–14 / 15–22 / >22 mmol/L:** <0.6 — usual dose / 5% TDD / 10% TDD · 0.6–0.9 — 5% (ensure oral intake) / 5% / 10% · 1–1.4 — 5% plus extra oral carbs / 10% / 10% · 1.5–2.9 — 5% plus extra carbs / 10% / **20%** · ≥3 — 10% plus extra carbs / **20% / 20%**, checked 1–2-hourly (2-hourly below 3). [S1]
- **Repeat corrections every 2–4 hours as needed**; if eating, give the usual carbohydrate insulin plus the correction; **continue the usual long-acting insulin.** [S1]
- **TDD = all rapid- and long-acting insulin in a day.** If dosing is flexible or the family is unsure, **estimate 1 unit/kg** from the most recent weight. [S1]

## Insulin pumps

- **BGL >15 with ketones <0.6: continue basal insulin and give the correction bolus through the pump** (with the carbohydrate bolus if eating). [S1]
- ⚠️ **BGL >15 with ketones ≥0.6: assume pump line failure and disconnect.** Give the correction (and any carbohydrate bolus) by subcutaneous injection, change the cannula, then resume basal insulin. [S1]
- **Any concern about pump delivery: change to subcutaneous injections**, with the usual diabetes team. [S1]
- **If possible, talk to the child's usual diabetes team** about further insulin adjustments. [S1]
