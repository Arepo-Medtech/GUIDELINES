# Glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype) in adults

**Edition:** 2.0 · 2026-09-24 · **Status:** drafted and source-checked; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** imaging, classification and molecular testing, surgery, chemoradiation with temozolomide, tumour-treating fields, response monitoring, recurrence and supportive care for adults with glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype, WHO grade 4). IDH-mutant astrocytoma and oligodendroglioma: see `idh-mutant-glioma`. Paediatric high- and low-grade glioma (dabrafenib/trametinib) are not covered. NTRK-fusion gliomas: see `ntrk-fusion-solid-tumours`. PBS access for each drug is in `docs/no-guideline-pbs-listings.md`.

> ✅ **COMPILED FROM 2 SOURCES.** 30 claims; **54 fragments or anchors re-checkable by machine; 8 doses.** **S1** Canadian multidisciplinary expert panel (published 1 April 2025; international): 21 claims, quoted · **S2** eviQ Cancer Treatments Online (current versions retrieved 24 September 2026; AU): 9 claims, paraphrased, hash-anchored

> ⚠️ International guideline (Canadian expert Delphi consensus, 2025) for S1. Australian practice follows the PBS listings below and may differ.

> 🇦🇺 **S2 is Australian: four eviQ treatment protocols (365, 366, 2033, 3363).** They are regimen protocols, not guidelines, and add PBS status, blood-count rules, PJP prophylaxis and lomustine dosing.

> ⚠️ **Lomustine, which S1 offers at recurrence, is no longer TGA-registered or PBS-listed in Australia (S2).**

> ⚠️ **Carmustine is PBS-listed for glioblastoma but this guideline does not discuss it;** its PBS-listed treatments are temozolomide and carmustine. Tumour-treating fields and bevacizumab, which the guideline covers, are not in the PBS data below.

## Recognise and diagnose

- **Glioblastoma has no cure;** in Canada median survival was 8.1 months and 5-year survival 4.9%. [S1]
- **Image every suspected glioblastoma with brain MRI;** use CT only if MRI is unavailable or contraindicated. Advanced MRI and PET help in difficult cases. [S1]
- **Under WHO 2021, only IDH-wildtype grade 4 gliomas are glioblastoma.** An adult IDH-wildtype astrocytic glioma counts as glioblastoma if it shows microvascular proliferation, necrosis, TERT promoter mutation, EGFR amplification or +7/−10 changes. [S1]
- **Test every new glioma for IDH mutation,** and test MGMT promoter methylation in glioblastoma to inform prognosis and predict temozolomide benefit. [S1]

## Plan treatment

- **Management is palliative from the outset:** discuss goals of care with patient and family; palliative care alone is reasonable when performance status is very poor. [S1]
- **Base first-line choice on tumour size, location and integrated diagnosis, and on age, KPS, symptoms and preferences,** within a multidisciplinary team. [S1]
- **Maximal safe resection for operable tumours with good performance status;** stereotactic biopsy when resection is not possible, and bank tissue where feasible. [S1]

## First-line chemoradiation

- **Start radiotherapy as soon as safe, ideally 3–6 weeks after surgery.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Age 70 or under with KPS 60 or more:** 60 Gy radiotherapy with concurrent temozolomide 75 mg/m² for 42 days, then adjuvant temozolomide 150–200 mg/m² on 5 of every 28 days for six cycles. [S1]
- **Temozolomide benefit is concentrated in MGMT-methylated tumours;** extending adjuvant temozolomide beyond six cycles did not improve survival in a recent meta-analysis, and the panel did not agree on 12 months. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Over 70 with KPS 60 or more:** 40 Gy hypofractionated radiotherapy with temozolomide 75 mg/m² for 21 days, then adjuvant temozolomide 150–200 mg/m² (5 of 28 days) for 6–12 cycles. Older patients with truly unmethylated MGMT may gain nothing from temozolomide. [S1]
- **KPS under 60:** hypofractionated radiotherapy (25 Gy/5, 34 Gy/10 or 40 Gy/15) with or without temozolomide, temozolomide alone only if MGMT-methylated, or palliative care alone. [S1]
- **Tumour-treating fields can be added to adjuvant temozolomide** after chemoradiation for KPS 60 or more, whatever the MGMT status; EF-14 improved median survival from 16.0 to 20.9 months. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Concurrent phase (eviQ):** temozolomide 75 mg/m² by mouth every day from the first to the last day of radiotherapy, weekends included (usually 6 weeks; the trial allowed up to 49 days). With radiotherapy it is a PBS restricted benefit. [S2]
- ⚠️ **PJP prophylaxis (eviQ):** recommended for everyone on temozolomide with concurrent radiotherapy, whatever the lymphocyte count, until treatment ends and counts recover. In the adjuvant phase it is the treating clinician's call, weighing lymphopenia and high-dose steroids. [S2]
- **Counts during the concurrent phase:** FBC, EUC, eGFR and liver tests at baseline and every second week. Hold temozolomide for neutrophils 0.5 to under 1.5 or platelets 25 to under 100 x 10⁹/L and resume at the same dose on recovery; stop it for neutrophils under 0.5, febrile neutropenia or platelets under 25. [S2]
- ⚠️ **Adjuvant phase (eviQ):** temozolomide 150 mg/m² on days 1–5 of cycle 1, rising to 200 mg/m² from cycle 2 if cycle 1 was well tolerated, every 28 days for 6 cycles (up to 12 at the clinician's discretion); dose level −1 is 100 mg/m². It is on the PBS general schedule. Do not change the dexamethasone dose in a temozolomide week. [S2]
- **Counts in the adjuvant phase:** FBC, EUC, eGFR and liver tests before each cycle. Delay for neutrophils 1.0 to under 1.5 or platelets 50 to under 100 and restart at the same dose; for neutrophils under 1.0, platelets under 50 or febrile neutropenia, delay and drop one dose level. [S2]
- **Stupp trial, 5-year results:** survival with temozolomide was 27.2% at 2 years and 9.8% at 5 years, against 10.9% and 1.9% with radiotherapy alone (HR 0.6), with benefit in every prognostic group including ages 60–70. [S2]
- ⚠️ **Older patients, short course (eviQ):** temozolomide 75 mg/m² daily for 21 days with 3 weeks of radiotherapy, for newly diagnosed older patients unsuited to standard chemoradiation (ECOG 0–2; caution in the very old), stopping temozolomide if radiotherapy stops. Median survival was 9.3 months against 7.6 with radiotherapy alone (HR 0.67). [S2]

## Monitor

- **MRI 4–6 weeks after radiotherapy, then every 2–4 months on treatment;** assess recurrence with gadolinium MRI and RANO criteria. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Beware pseudoprogression:** do not call progression on MRI in the first 12 weeks after radiotherapy unless new enhancement lies outside the field or pathology confirms tumour; keep adjuvant temozolomide going when pseudoprogression is suspected. [S1]

## Recurrence

- **No standard of care exists at recurrence:** individualise, and encourage clinical trial entry for everyone. [S1]
- **Reoperate only with KPS over 70 and a favourable tumour location;** re-irradiation suits selected small, favourably placed tumours long after prior treatment. [S1]
- **Lomustine or temozolomide rechallenge may help some patients;** bevacizumab may be added to chemotherapy in selected patients, but adding it to lomustine did not improve survival. [S1]
- **Consider targeted therapy or immunotherapy for actionable alterations** such as BRAF V600E, NTRK fusions or MSI-high. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Lomustine at recurrence (eviQ):** 110 mg/m² by mouth on day 1 every 6 weeks until progression or toxicity, a committee-consensus dose chosen for toxicity in heavily pretreated patients; consider 70 mg/m² after significant thrombocytopenia on temozolomide. It is no longer TGA-registered and not PBS-listed. [S2]
- ⚠️ **Lomustine safety:** counts reach delayed nadirs, so cut later cycles by 25% or 50% for low neutrophils or platelets. Use caution if FVC or DLCO is below 70% of predicted, and do lung function tests at baseline and during treatment: pulmonary fibrosis is dose-related, commoner above a cumulative 1,100 mg/m², and can appear up to 17 years later. [S2]

## Supportive care

- **Treat seizures with antiepileptic drugs** (e.g. levetiracetam, lacosamide, perampanel), and screen early for depression and refer; consider rehabilitation. [S1]
- **The panel did not agree on PJP prophylaxis during temozolomide (23%) or LMWH VTE prophylaxis after resection (46%).** [S1]
