## Test and stage

- **KRAS is mutated in more than 90% of these cancers.** Test somatic KRAS in unresectable disease and germline BRCA in all patients; somatic BRCA testing can be considered. BRCA carriers do better on platinum and may benefit from PARP inhibitors. [S1]
- **KRAS wild-type tumours can carry other actionable changes** (NTRK, ROS1, ALK, RET), so they warrant wider testing where NGS is limited. [S1]

## Resectable disease

- **Surgery should remove and examine at least 16 lymph nodes** for adequate staging, with perioperative heparin thromboprophylaxis (unfractionated or LMWH) unless contraindicated. [S1]
- **Drain the biliary tree endoscopically before resection only if bilirubin exceeds 250 μmol/l with cholangitis, or surgery will be delayed beyond 2 weeks.** [S1]
- **For resectable tumours, avoid neoadjuvant treatment outside trials:** phase III evidence is too thin. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Adjuvant mFOLFIRINOX for resected patients with ECOG PS 0–1** (median survival 54.4 vs 35.0 months with gemcitabine); S-1 is an Asian alternative. If mFOLFIRINOX is unsuitable, gemcitabine–capecitabine; failing that, gemcitabine or 5-FU–leucovorin alone. [S1]

## Borderline resectable and locally advanced disease

- **Borderline resectable: induction FOLFIRINOX (or modified) or gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel, then surgery.** Gemcitabine doublets are options when those are not feasible. [S1]
- **Locally advanced: up to 6 months of combination chemotherapy aiming at conversion surgery** is a valid strategy; surgery is discussed if CA 19-9 falls markedly, the patient improves and the tumour downstages. [S1]

## Advanced disease

- ⚠️ **First line with ECOG PS 0–1 and bilirubin under 1.5 times normal: FOLFIRINOX, NALIRIFOX, gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel or mFOLFIRINOX.** With PS 2, KPS under 70 or higher bilirubin: gemcitabine (or S-1) alone. [S1]
- **Gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel (MPACT, KPS 70 or more):** response 23% vs 7% and median survival 8.7 vs 6.6 months against gemcitabine. A meta-analysis found overall survival similar to FOLFIRINOX. [S1]
- **NALIRIFOX beat gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel on survival (11.1 vs 9.2 months) in the NAPOLI trial;** a network meta-analysis favours NALIRIFOX or FOLFIRINOX when tolerable, with gemcitabine–nab-paclitaxel a sound alternative. [S1]
- **Second line after FOLFIRINOX: gemcitabine alone or with paclitaxel or nab-paclitaxel** (PS 0–2). After gemcitabine-based treatment, with PS 0–1: liposomal irinotecan–5-FU–leucovorin or mFOLFIRINOX. Oxaliplatin-based second line is contentious. [S1]
- **NTRK fusion: larotrectinib, entrectinib or repotrectinib.** [S1]

## Supportive care and follow-up

- ⚠️ **Venous thromboembolism affects 20%–25% of patients;** consider primary thromboprophylaxis during chemotherapy for advanced disease if there is no contraindication. [S1]
- **Duodenal obstruction: an endoscopic metal stent, or EUS-guided gastroenterostomy where expertise exists,** rather than surgery. [S1]
- **Regular follow-up after resection is now suggested:** routine imaging was linked to longer survival than symptom-led follow-up (28 vs 23 months) in a prospective study. [S1]
