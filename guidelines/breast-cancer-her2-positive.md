# Breast cancer — HER2-positive early disease: neoadjuvant TCHP

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-anchored; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** neoadjuvant docetaxel, carboplatin, trastuzumab and pertuzumab (TCHP) for adults with operable HER2-positive early breast cancer, and what continues after surgery: eligibility, dosing, cardiac and blood monitoring, dose changes and the evidence. It is authored from **one eviQ protocol page (ID 3736)**, because eviQ publishes regimens, not a treatment-sequence overview. **Metastatic HER2-positive disease is not covered** (for example docetaxel, pertuzumab and trastuzumab, eviQ ID 1596; trastuzumab deruxtecan, ID 4150; trastuzumab emtansine, ID 1598). HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced disease: see `breast-cancer-hormone-receptor-positive`. PBS access for each drug is in `docs/no-guideline-pbs-listings.md`.

## Who it is for

- **TCHP is given before surgery for operable HER2-positive early breast cancer**, with HER2 status confirmed by in situ hybridisation (ISH). [S1]
- ⚠️ **Do not give if LVEF is 45% or less;** take care if ECOG performance status is 2 or worse. [S1]

## Regimen

- **Every 21 days for 6 cycles before surgery**, all on day 1: pertuzumab 840 mg loading then 420 mg; trastuzumab 8 mg/kg loading then 6 mg/kg; docetaxel 75 mg/m²; and carboplatin AUC 6. Pegfilgrastim 6 mg is injected under the skin on day 2. [S1]
- **Subcutaneous trastuzumab 600 mg every three weeks is an acceptable alternative** to the IV form (similar safety, non-inferior pharmacokinetics and efficacy). **Never substitute trastuzumab emtansine or trastuzumab deruxtecan for trastuzumab:** the drugs are not interchangeable. [S1]
- **Carboplatin AUC 6 was the trial dose; the physician may lower it (such as AUC 5).** Measured GFR is preferred in the Calvert formula when treatment is curative, and is strongly advised when estimated GFR exceeds 125 mL/min. [S1]
- **After surgery, the response decides what continues:** trastuzumab with or without pertuzumab to complete 1 year in total, or trastuzumab emtansine for 14 cycles, with adjuvant endocrine therapy added for hormone receptor-positive disease. [S1]
- **PBS:** docetaxel and carboplatin are general schedule items; pertuzumab, trastuzumab and pegfilgrastim are authority items. [S1]
- **Emetic risk is moderate:** prophylaxis combines an NK1 antagonist, a 5HT3 antagonist and a steroid (PBS-subsidised), and patients also take home metoclopramide 10 mg or prochlorperazine 10 mg for breakthrough nausea. [S1]

## Monitoring

- ⚠️ **Cardiac monitoring:** HER2-directed drugs raise the risk of falling LVEF and heart failure. Test cardiac function at baseline and every 3 months on treatment, and avoid long periods of concurrent anthracycline and HER2 therapy. [S1]
- **Bloods:** LFTs, FBC and EUC/eGFR at baseline and before every cycle, and calcium and magnesium at baseline. Recalculate the carboplatin dose only if kidney function moves by more than 20% or the patient's condition changes. [S1]
- **Screen for hepatitis B** (HBsAg, anti-HBc, anti-HBs) before starting; prophylaxis follows individual risk and local policy. [S1]
- **No live vaccines** for moderately or severely immunocompromised patients; non-live vaccines are safe, though the response may be weaker. [S1]

## Dose changes and red flags

- ⚠️ **Falling LVEF:** at 40% to 45%, or a drop of 10 points or more from baseline, delay trastuzumab and pertuzumab, repeat LVEF within 3 weeks and consider cardiology referral; consider stopping if it has not recovered to within 10 points. Stop if LVEF below 40% is confirmed, or for symptomatic heart failure. [S1]
- **Pertuzumab and trastuzumab doses are never reduced,** and pertuzumab is stopped whenever trastuzumab is stopped. [S1]
- **Missed doses:** if a trastuzumab or pertuzumab dose is 6 weeks late or less, give it (pertuzumab 420 mg) as soon as possible. If more than 6 weeks late, reload (trastuzumab 8 mg/kg, pertuzumab 840 mg) and then resume the 3-weekly doses (6 mg/kg and 420 mg). [S1]
- **Neutrophils:** for ANC 0.5 to under 1.0, delay until recovery; for ANC under 0.5 or febrile neutropenia, delay and consider cutting docetaxel and carboplatin by 25% for later cycles. [S1]
- **Docetaxel:** check for neuropathy and for fluid retention or unexplained weight gain before each cycle. Persistent grade 2 neuropathy means cutting docetaxel by 25% and then 50%; grade 3 or 4 means omitting it. With hepatic dysfunction, cut docetaxel by 25% or 50%, or omit it if moderate or severe. [S1]

## Evidence

- **The evidence is limited.** eviQ found no strong evidence for neoadjuvant TCHP, and its panel leaned mainly on the phase II TRYPHAENA trial. There, TCHP gave a pathological complete response in 66.2%, against 61.6% and 57.3% with the anthracycline-containing arms, with little symptomatic left ventricular dysfunction. [S1]
- **At 3 years in TRYPHAENA,** disease-free survival was 87% to 90% and overall survival 93% to 94% across the arms. A pCR predicted remaining disease-free (HR 0.27). Neutropenia and diarrhoea are the most clinically important toxicities. [S1]
