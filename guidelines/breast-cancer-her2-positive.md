# Breast cancer — HER2-positive early disease: neoadjuvant TCHP

**Edition:** 2.0 · 2026-09-24 · **Status:** drafted and source-checked; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** neoadjuvant docetaxel, carboplatin, trastuzumab and pertuzumab (TCHP) for adults with operable HER2-positive early breast cancer, and what continues after surgery: eligibility, dosing, cardiac and blood monitoring, dose changes and the evidence. It is authored from **one eviQ protocol page (ID 3736)**, because eviQ publishes regimens, not a treatment-sequence overview. **Metastatic HER2-positive disease is not covered** (for example docetaxel, pertuzumab and trastuzumab, eviQ ID 1596; trastuzumab deruxtecan, ID 4150; trastuzumab emtansine, ID 1598). HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced disease: see `breast-cancer-hormone-receptor-positive`. PBS access for each drug is in `docs/no-guideline-pbs-listings.md`. Edition 2.0 adds, from S2 (SEOM-GEICAM-SOLTI 2025), the wider early HER2-positive pathway: choosing a neoadjuvant regimen, adjuvant treatment after upfront surgery, and treatment after neoadjuvant therapy.

> ⚠️ **Early disease only.** This guideline is written from the eviQ neoadjuvant TCHP protocol. Most PBS items for HER2-positive breast cancer also cover metastatic use, which is not covered here.

> ⚠️ **eviQ labels this protocol as based on limited evidence** (a phase II trial), although it is endorsed for use.

## Who it is for

- **TCHP is given before surgery for operable HER2-positive early breast cancer**, with HER2 status confirmed by in situ hybridisation (ISH). [S1]
- ⚠️ **Do not give if LVEF is 45% or less;** take care if ECOG performance status is 2 or worse. [S1]

## Regimen

- **Every 21 days for 6 cycles before surgery**, all on day 1: pertuzumab 840 mg loading then 420 mg; trastuzumab 8 mg/kg loading then 6 mg/kg; docetaxel 75 mg/m²; and carboplatin AUC 6. Pegfilgrastim 6 mg is injected under the skin on day 2. [S1]
- **Subcutaneous trastuzumab 600 mg every three weeks is an acceptable alternative** to the IV form (similar safety, non-inferior pharmacokinetics and efficacy). **Never substitute trastuzumab emtansine or trastuzumab deruxtecan for trastuzumab:** the drugs are not interchangeable. [S1]
- **Carboplatin AUC 6 was the trial dose; the physician may lower it (such as AUC 5).** Measured GFR is preferred in the Calvert formula when treatment is curative, and is strongly advised when estimated GFR exceeds 125 mL/min. [S1]
- **After surgery, the response decides what continues:** trastuzumab with or without pertuzumab to complete 1 year in total, or trastuzumab emtansine for 14 cycles, with adjuvant endocrine therapy added for hormone receptor-positive disease. [S1]
- **PBS:** docetaxel and carboplatin are general schedule items; pertuzumab, trastuzumab and pegfilgrastim are authority items. [S1]
- **Emetic risk is moderate:** prophylaxis combines an NK1 antagonist, a 5HT3 antagonist and a steroid (PBS-subsidised), and patients also take home metoclopramide 10 mg or prochlorperazine 10 mg for breakthrough nausea. [S1]

## Monitoring

- ⚠️ **Cardiac monitoring:** HER2-directed drugs raise the risk of falling LVEF and heart failure. Test cardiac function at baseline and every 3 months on treatment, and avoid long periods of concurrent anthracycline and HER2 therapy. [S1]
- **Bloods:** LFTs, FBC and EUC/eGFR at baseline and before every cycle, and calcium and magnesium at baseline. Recalculate the carboplatin dose only if kidney function moves by more than 20% or the patient's condition changes. [S1]
- **Screen for hepatitis B** (HBsAg, anti-HBc, anti-HBs) before starting; prophylaxis follows individual risk and local policy. [S1]
- **No live vaccines** for moderately or severely immunocompromised patients; non-live vaccines are safe, though the response may be weaker. [S1]

## Dose changes and red flags

- ⚠️ **Falling LVEF:** at 40% to 45%, or a drop of 10 points or more from baseline, delay trastuzumab and pertuzumab, repeat LVEF within 3 weeks and consider cardiology referral; consider stopping if it has not recovered to within 10 points. Stop if LVEF below 40% is confirmed, or for symptomatic heart failure. [S1]
- **Pertuzumab and trastuzumab doses are never reduced,** and pertuzumab is stopped whenever trastuzumab is stopped. [S1]
- **Missed doses:** if a trastuzumab or pertuzumab dose is 6 weeks late or less, give it (pertuzumab 420 mg) as soon as possible. If more than 6 weeks late, reload (trastuzumab 8 mg/kg, pertuzumab 840 mg) and then resume the 3-weekly doses (6 mg/kg and 420 mg). [S1]
- **Neutrophils:** for ANC 0.5 to under 1.0, delay until recovery; for ANC under 0.5 or febrile neutropenia, delay and consider cutting docetaxel and carboplatin by 25% for later cycles. [S1]
- **Docetaxel:** check for neuropathy and for fluid retention or unexplained weight gain before each cycle. Persistent grade 2 neuropathy means cutting docetaxel by 25% and then 50%; grade 3 or 4 means omitting it. With hepatic dysfunction, cut docetaxel by 25% or 50%, or omit it if moderate or severe. [S1]

## Choosing treatment across early HER2-positive disease (S2)

- **Neoadjuvant treatment is the route for tumours cT2 or larger, or node-positive:** chemotherapy (a taxane with AC or EC, or a taxane with or without carboplatin) plus dual blockade with pertuzumab and trastuzumab [I, A]. The response to it is a surrogate for disease-free survival and decides the adjuvant plan. [S2]
- **Two neoadjuvant backbones are established:** an anthracycline then taxane sequence, or docetaxel with carboplatin, each with pertuzumab and trastuzumab and giving at least 9 weeks of taxane with anti-HER2 therapy. In TRAIN-2 the anthracycline did not improve pCR or long-term outcomes over the taxane-platinum regimen, and LVEF declines are less common without it, so the anthracycline-free option suits patients where cardiotoxicity is a concern [II, B]. [S2]
- ⚠️ **Residual disease after neoadjuvant therapy** (at least 6 cycles, including 9 weeks or more of taxane with trastuzumab): switch to trastuzumab emtansine for 14 cycles. In KATHERINE it beat trastuzumab, with 7-year iDFS 80.8% against 67.1% and overall survival 89.1% against 84.4% [I, A]. [S2]
- **After a pCR:** continue trastuzumab until a full year of anti-HER2 therapy is reached [I, A]; if nodes were positive at diagnosis, pertuzumab may be added, per APHINITY [II, B]. [S2]
- **Adjuvant therapy after upfront surgery:** trastuzumab for 12 months with chemotherapy, for every tumour over 1 cm whatever the nodes [I, A], and worth considering in node-negative 0.5–1 cm tumours, particularly ER-negative ones [II, B]. Preferred regimens: AC or EC for four cycles then 3 months of paclitaxel or docetaxel with trastuzumab, or docetaxel, carboplatin and trastuzumab (TCH) [I, A]. It can run alongside radiotherapy or endocrine therapy. [S2]
- **Stage I disease:** consider 12 weeks of paclitaxel with trastuzumab, then trastuzumab alone to complete 1 year (APT trial) [II, B]. [S2]
- **Adjuvant pertuzumab (APHINITY)** gave a modest but significant gain in iDFS and overall survival in node-positive patients who had not had neoadjuvant treatment; the EMA approved 18 cycles of trastuzumab with pertuzumab for high-risk node-positive disease, whether started before or after surgery [I, A]. Spain does not fund the adjuvant indication. [S2]
- **Extended adjuvant neratinib:** 1 year of neratinib after a year of trastuzumab improved iDFS in ExteNET, most in HR-positive, node-positive disease, at the cost of more diarrhoea [I, B]. The EMA limits it to HR-positive disease. [S2]
- **HER2-positive, HR-positive disease:** add adjuvant endocrine therapy on the same principles as for HER2-negative disease. Nothing supports adding a CDK4/6 inhibitor or a PARP inhibitor after surgery in this subtype. [S2]
- **Before anthracyclines or HER2-targeted drugs, assess cardiac function** [I, A]. The HER2DX 27-gene test may help choose escalation or de-escalation around dual blockade, but prospective validation is still under way [II, B]. [S2]

## Evidence

- **The evidence is limited.** eviQ found no strong evidence for neoadjuvant TCHP, and its panel leaned mainly on the phase II TRYPHAENA trial. There, TCHP gave a pathological complete response in 66.2%, against 61.6% and 57.3% with the anthracycline-containing arms, with little symptomatic left ventricular dysfunction. [S1]
- **At 3 years in TRYPHAENA,** disease-free survival was 87% to 90% and overall survival 93% to 94% across the arms. A pCR predicted remaining disease-free (HR 0.27). Neutropenia and diarrhoea are the most clinically important toxicities. [S1]
