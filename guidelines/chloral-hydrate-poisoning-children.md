# Chloral hydrate poisoning in children

**Edition:** 1.1 · 2026-09-24 · **Status:** drafted and source-anchored; awaiting clinical attestation

## Key points

- ⚠️ **Chloral hydrate has caused deaths, mainly in outpatient use and usually at doses over 10 g**, although it is used safely for in-hospital sedation. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Narrow therapeutic index: recommended dose 10–50 mg/kg, max 2 g. Severe toxicity is seen over 100 mg/kg.** [S1]
- **Toxicity is rapid (under 30 minutes): cardiac dysrhythmias and CNS depression.** High doses may corrode mucosa. Peak levels at 30–60 minutes. [S1]

## Dose limits

- ⚠️ **Take care with repeated doses over a short period: no more than 100 mg/kg per procedure and no more than 200 mg/kg in 24 hours.** Higher doses are associated with coma and dysrhythmias. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Acute overdose over 10 g → risk of gastric perforation, haemorrhage and strictures.** [S1]

## Who needs assessment

- **Assess:** all deliberate self-poisonings · any significant accidental ingestion · any patient with symptoms · **any dose over 50 mg/kg.** Consider non-accidental poisoning if the developmental age doesn't fit. [S1]
- **Look for:** ataxia and drowsiness **that quickly progress to coma** · respiratory depression · hypothermia · hypotension · corrosive GI injury. [S1]
- **ECG** for multifocal PVCs, AF, SVT or VT. **Consider endoscopy within 24 hours** after ingestions over 10 g with signs of corrosive injury. Every intentional overdose needs a paracetamol level. [S1]

## Management

- ⚠️ **Intubate early if CNS depression is progressing or there is cardiovascular toxicity.** [S1]
- **Dysrhythmias: beta blocker (esmolol or propranolol).** For torsades, MgSO₄ can be added, though it may not work. VT: lignocaine may work. [S1]
- **Hypotension: IV 0.9% saline 20 mL/kg.** ⚠️ **Catecholamine inotropes are contraindicated** — they may precipitate arrhythmias. [S1]
- ⚠️ **No charcoal unless the airway is protected.** **Consider haemodialysis** for ongoing haemodynamic instability or arrhythmias. [S1]

## Disposition

- **Consider admission for every adolescent with an intentional overdose**; assess self-harm risk before ward transfer, but **don't let it delay urgent care such as ICU.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Cardiovascular depression or arrhythmias: manage in a paediatric ICU** — PIPER 1300 137 650. [S1]
- **Discharge:** observe symptomatic patients until asymptomatic; normal GCS; normal ECG; **at least 2 hours of cardiac monitoring**; after deliberate ingestion, a risk assessment showing low risk of further self-harm. [S1]
