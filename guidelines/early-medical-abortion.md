# Early medical abortion

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-anchored; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** medical abortion with mifepristone and misoprostol in early pregnancy (up to 9 weeks in Australia): information and choice, pre-abortion testing, the regimen, pain relief, follow-up to exclude ongoing pregnancy, incomplete abortion and contraception afterwards. Surgical abortion, medical abortion after 10 weeks and feticide are summarised only in Unresolved. Contraception in general: `contraception` (which excludes abortion care) and `contraception-adolescents`.

> ✅ **PARAPHRASED, HASH-ANCHORED.** 23 claims paraphrased from **Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (RANZCOG) — *Clinical Guideline for Abortion Care: An evidence-based guideline on abortion care in Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand (version 1.4)*** (2026 (v1.4; first published 2023); origin: AU). **33 anchors re-checkable by machine; 6 doses.** The source's words are not reproduced: its licence is *© RANZCOG, all rights reserved; parts may be reproduced by clinicians, hospitals and medical and allied health organisations without permission, with acknowledgement*.

> ⚠️ **Australia differs from New Zealand on the gestation limit.** The source's recommendations are written for up to 10 weeks, but in Australia MS-2 Step is TGA-approved only **up to 9 weeks (63 days)** and only for **buccal** misoprostol. Beyond that is off-label here.

## Before the abortion

- **Give information first:** which tests may be needed, which methods suit the gestation, the benefits and drawbacks of each, what pain and bleeding to expect, the possible complications (including incomplete abortion, ongoing pregnancy and pelvic infection) and contraception afterwards. Explain that abortion is not associated with infertility, cancer or mental-health problems. (Conditional, low certainty) [S1]
- **Women should choose the method they find most acceptable,** free of coercion, once properly informed. Up to 14 weeks, medical and surgical abortion are both safe, effective and acceptable, so a choice can be offered. (Conditional, moderate certainty) [S1]
- **Every part of an early medical abortion service can be delivered by telehealth or face to face.** (Conditional, low certainty) Passing the pregnancy at home can be offered up to 10+0 weeks, depending on preference, clinical need and quick access to urgent care. [S1]
- **Only offer medical abortion where timely medical care can be reached.** Have a clear pathway for retrieval if complications occur (for example aeromedical); in remote areas, inpatient care may suit better. [S1]
- **A clinician with a conscientious objection must say so and tell the woman how to reach the nearest provider** within a clinically reasonable time; the objection must not cause delay, distress or harm. [S1]

## Tests beforehand

- **Haemoglobin and Rh D blood group do not need routine testing** before abortion up to 10 weeks. Use clinical judgement to test selectively where bleeding risk is higher, such as anaemia or a later gestation. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Give anti-D to Rh D negative women whose pregnancy is 10 weeks or more.** [S1]
- **Establish gestational age first,** either clinically (a history that covers the last period, examining or not) or by ultrasound. (Conditional, very low certainty) ⚠️ **Scan when the clinical dating is uncertain, or if symptoms or signs suggest ectopic pregnancy** or another concern; otherwise the decision to scan follows patient preference and service access. [S1]
- **Offer STI screening plus HIV, hepatitis B and syphilis testing to every woman having an abortion,** but do not let it delay care: same-day abortion takes priority. Treat positives and notify partners per local sexual-health guidance. [S1]

## The regimen

- ⚠️ **Mifepristone 200 mg by mouth, then misoprostol 800 mcg buccally, sublingually or vaginally 24–48 hours later.** (Strong recommendation, moderate certainty) Local regulation governs which regimen is used. [S1]
- ⚠️ **In Australia, early medical abortion is provided up to 9 weeks (63 days)** using the MS-2 Step composite pack (1 × 200 mg mifepristone plus 4 × 200 mcg misoprostol), and **buccal is the only approved misoprostol route here.** New Zealand works to 10 weeks (70 days). [S1]
- **Why this combination:** mifepristone plus misoprostol fails less often than misoprostol alone; 800 mcg is probably the most effective misoprostol dose, and a 24–48 hour gap after mifepristone works best. Buccal or vaginal use leaves fewer ongoing pregnancies than swallowing it. [S1]
- **Offer an antiemetic and analgesia.** [S1]
- **Ibuprofen for pain (up to 14 weeks):** one 1600 mg dose (off-label), then 400–600 mg every eight hours, to a ceiling of 2400 mg in 24 hours while pain continues. (Strong, moderate certainty) [S1]
- **Paracetamol can be added:** 1000 mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed, 4000 mg per 24 hours at most, with an antiemetic given 30 minutes before the misoprostol. Opioids only selectively. [S1]
- **No routine antibiotic prophylaxis for medical abortion up to 14 weeks:** serious infection is very unlikely (<1%) and antibiotics carry side effects and resistance risk. (Conditional, very low certainty) [S1]

## Follow-up

- **Offer follow-up to rule out an ongoing pregnancy and check for complications;** symptoms alone cannot reliably exclude it. It can be in person, by telehealth, or self-assessed with a urine test. [S1]
- **Urine test:** a negative low-sensitivity urine pregnancy test 14–21 days after mifepristone excludes ongoing pregnancy. **Serum test:** a β-hCG fall of 80% or more, from a level taken within 72 hours of mifepristone to one taken 8–16 days later, excludes it. A positive, invalid or smaller fall needs further investigation. [S1]

## When it does not complete

- **Incomplete early medical abortion:** options are surgical evacuation, another misoprostol dose, or waiting, chosen by the woman's preference, her symptoms and stability, and access to surgery. (Conditional, very low certainty) [S1]
- **Misoprostol for incomplete abortion:** 800 mcg buccally, repeated as 400 mcg 4 hours later if tissue has not passed. No scan is needed before misoprostol, but one is usually done before surgical evacuation unless bleeding is heavy. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Ongoing viable pregnancy:** offer a repeat medical or surgical abortion by preference and access, and warn of the small rise in teratogenic risk from misoprostol if the pregnancy is continued. [S1]

## Contraception afterwards

- **Intrauterine contraception:** insert as soon as possible once the pregnancy has passed after medical abortion (or at the time of surgical abortion). **Implant:** insert on the day mifepristone is taken. (Strong, low certainty) [S1]
- **Depot medroxyprogesterone acetate can be given at the time of medical abortion,** even before expulsion, once the small possible added risk of ongoing pregnancy has been discussed. [S1]
