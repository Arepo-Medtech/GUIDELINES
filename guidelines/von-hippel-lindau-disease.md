# Von Hippel-Lindau disease: belzutifan for VHL-associated tumours

**Edition:** 1.0 · 2026-09-23 · **Status:** drafted and source-anchored; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** belzutifan for adults with von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) disease whose renal cell carcinoma, CNS haemangioblastoma or pancreatic neuroendocrine tumour does not need immediate surgery: eligibility, dose, dose reductions, hypoxia and anaemia, monitoring, interactions and the LITESPARK-004 evidence. VHL genetic testing and lifelong tumour surveillance are separate eviQ pages (ID 615, ID 397) and are not covered. Advanced clear cell RCC in general: see `renal-cell-carcinoma` (which does not cover belzutifan). Sporadic pancreatic NETs: see `neuroendocrine-neoplasms`. PBS access for each drug is in `docs/no-guideline-pbs-listings.md`.

## Who it is for

- **VHL-associated tumours belong with an experienced multidisciplinary team** that knows this disease. [S1]
- **First confirm the diagnosis of VHL disease:** a germline VHL alteration, or two or more hallmark VHL manifestations, or one hallmark manifestation plus a documented family history. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Belzutifan is for VHL tumours that do not need surgery now:** non-metastatic renal cell carcinoma, CNS haemangioblastoma, or non-metastatic pancreatic NET, in a patient with ECOG performance status 0-1. [S1]
- **In Australia the PBS funds belzutifan only for VHL-associated disease.** It is TGA-approved for pretreated advanced clear cell RCC (after a PD-1/L1 checkpoint inhibitor and VEGF-targeted therapy), but that use is not PBS-listed. [S1]

## Dose and administration

- ⚠️ **Belzutifan 120 mg orally once daily, continuously, until the disease progresses or toxicity becomes unacceptable.** Tablets are 40 mg. [S1]
- **Dose reductions go 120 → 80 → 40 mg once daily;** stop for good if the patient cannot manage after 2 reductions. [S1]
- **Take it at about the same time each day, swallowed whole with water (not crushed or chewed), with or without food.** A missed dose can be taken later the same day; after vomiting, do not repeat the dose, just take the next one as usual. [S1]
- **Nausea risk is minimal to low,** so routine antiemetic prophylaxis is not needed. [S1]

## Safety: hypoxia and anaemia

- ⚠️ **Belzutifan can cause severe hypoxia** needing oxygen or admission. Onset is most often within the first 4 to 6 weeks, though it can appear months later. Check pulse oximetry before starting, regularly during that early window, then periodically. [S1]
- **Hypoxia at grade 3 (resting saturation 88% or less):** if symptomatic, withhold until grade 2 or better and restart one dose level lower or stop; **grade 4 (life-threatening airway compromise) means permanent discontinuation.** [S1]
- ⚠️ **Severe anaemia from erythropoietin suppression is expected and may need transfusion,** usually within the first 3 months but sometimes much later. Patients with combined UGT2B17 and CYP2C19 deletions are at higher risk; genotyping is not routine but can be considered for unexplained severe anaemia. [S1]
- **Anaemia grade 1 to 2: carry on and correct iron, B12 or folate deficiency. Grade 3: withhold until grade 2 or better, then resume at the same or a lower dose. Grade 4: withhold, resume one level lower, and stop permanently if it recurs.** [S1]
- **Wounds heal poorly on belzutifan:** stop it at least 5 to 7 days before elective surgery and restart only once the wound has healed. [S1]
- **Other effects include fatigue, headache, dizziness, dyspnoea, joint and muscle pain, bowel upset, oedema, liver toxicity and hypertension;** control any existing hypertension before starting. [S1]

## Monitoring and interactions

- **Before starting, check a full blood count, kidney function (EUC, eGFR), liver tests, iron studies, B12 and folate.** Then consider repeating the blood count, kidney and liver tests fortnightly (every 2 weeks) for 6 weeks, monthly for the following 5 months, then 3-monthly. [S1]
- **No kidney dose change when eGFR is 30 or more;** below 30, and in moderate or severe liver impairment, use caution because these groups were not studied. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Belzutifan induces CYP3A4 and can make hormonal contraception fail;** anyone using it needs an added barrier method. Pregnancy testing should be considered before starting, and effective contraception is needed during and after treatment. [S1]
- **CYP2C19 or UGT2B17 inhibitors (such as azoles, omeprazole, fluoxetine, ibuprofen) can raise belzutifan toxicity; CYP2C19 inducers (such as rifampicin or St John's wort) can reduce its effect.** Avoid these where possible or monitor closely. [S1]

## Evidence

- **The evidence is limited:** eviQ's committee published the protocol on the strength of the LITESPARK-004 single-arm, open-label phase 2 trial. [S1]
- **At a median follow-up of nearly 50 months, objective responses were 67% for VHL renal tumours (41 of 61), 48% for CNS haemangioblastomas (24 of 50) and 91% for pancreatic NETs (20 of 22).** [S1]
- **All trial patients had some treatment-related side effect, and 18% had a grade 3 event;** anaemia and hypoxia were the key toxicities, and the one grade 3 hypoxia settled with interruption and dose reduction. [S1]
- **ESMO-MCBS score 3 out of 5** for VHL disease where local procedures are not immediately needed. [S1]
