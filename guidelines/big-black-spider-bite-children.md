# Big black spider (funnel-web) bite in children

**Edition:** 1.1 · 2026-09-24 · **Status:** drafted and source-anchored; awaiting clinical attestation
**Scope:** first aid, recognition of systemic envenoming, antivenom access and discharge after a big black spider bite, including possible Sydney funnel-web, in children (Victorian perspective). Redback bites are `redback-spider-bite-children`; snake antivenoms are `snakebite-antivenoms`, which does not cover funnel-web antivenom.

> ⚠️ **Possible Sydney funnel-web bite: apply a pressure immobilisation bandage and obtain funnel-web antivenom before the bandage comes off.**

---

## Risk

- ⚠️ **The Sydney funnel-web can cause lethal envenoming**, although Victoria has no recorded case of severe envenoming, even though some local spiders are of similar toxicity. [S1]
- **The Victorian funnel-web (Dandenong Ranges) is not significantly venomous** — bite-site pain, headache, nausea. **Beware collectors' Sydney funnel-webs**; other funnel-webs and mouse spiders reaching eastern Victoria may cause systemic symptoms. [S1]
- **Apart from redbacks, other Victorian spiders need no medical intervention or observation unless infection develops.** [S1]
- **Assess anyone in whom a Sydney funnel-web bite is possible, and any symptomatic patient.** [S1]

## Recognise envenoming

- **Only a small number of bites cause systemic envenoming; if it comes, it develops over 10 minutes to 4 hours.** Bite marks are usually seen. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Systemic features:** tingling around the mouth · abdominal pain, headache, nausea, vomiting · salivation, piloerection, sweating, lacrimation · hypertension and tachycardia, possibly followed by bradycardia and hypotension · cyanosis, pulmonary oedema, respiratory failure. [S1]
- **No investigations are needed initially.** [S1]

## First aid and treatment

- ⚠️ **Put on a pressure immobilisation bandage.** Where the history or examination points to envenoming, **keep it in place** and talk to the Poisons Information Centre. [S1]
- ⚠️ **No Victorian hospital stocks Sydney funnel-web antivenom** — contact the Poisons Information Centre. If the child has symptoms or a Sydney funnel-web cannot be excluded, **have 4 ampoules of the CSL antivenom for funnel-web spider on hand before the bandage comes off.** [S1]
- **Without antivenom, support organ by organ:** intubation with IPPV/PEEP for pulmonary oedema · alpha blockade for tachycardia and hypertension · hypotension — fluids, inotropes, atropine. [S1]

## Discharge

- **Possible Sydney funnel-web, or any symptoms: discharge only if asymptomatic 4 hours after the bite and at least 2 hours after first aid is removed.** [S1]
