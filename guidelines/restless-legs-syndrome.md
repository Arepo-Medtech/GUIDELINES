## First steps for everyone

- ⚠️ **Check iron in everyone with clinically significant RLS:** ferritin and transferrin saturation, ideally a morning sample with no iron supplements or iron-rich food for at least 24 hours beforehand. The result decides between oral and IV iron. [S1]
- **Iron thresholds for RLS are higher than for the general population.** Adults: replace iron (oral or IV) at ferritin ≤ 75 ng/mL or a transferrin saturation under 20%; use only IV iron at ferritin 75 to 100 ng/mL. In children, supplement when ferritin is < 50 ng/mL. These consensus thresholds are untested. [S1]
- **Start by removing aggravating factors:** alcohol, caffeine, antihistaminergic, serotonergic and antidopaminergic drugs, and untreated obstructive sleep apnoea. [S1]
- **RLS is common in pregnancy;** weigh the pregnancy safety of each option before prescribing. [S1]

## Adults: preferred treatments (strong recommendations)

- ⚠️ **Gabapentinoids are the preferred drugs:** gabapentin enacarbil, gabapentin and pregabalin each carry a strong recommendation (moderate certainty). [S1]
- **IV ferric carboxymaltose is strongly recommended** when iron status meets the thresholds above (moderate certainty). [S1]

## Adults: other options (conditional recommendations)

- **Other iron preparations are suggested when iron status fits:** IV low molecular weight iron dextran or IV ferumoxytol (very low certainty), or oral ferrous sulfate (moderate certainty). [S1]
- **Opioids, including extended-release oxycodone, are a suggested option** (moderate certainty); dipyridamole is also suggested (low certainty). [S1]
- **Bilateral high-frequency peroneal nerve stimulation is a suggested non-drug option** (moderate certainty). [S1]

## Dopamine agonists and levodopa

- ⚠️ **Pramipexole is not advised for standard use** (conditional, moderate certainty). It may still suit someone who values short-term symptom relief over long-term harms, especially augmentation. [S1]
- **The same applies to ropinirole (moderate certainty), transdermal rotigotine (low certainty) and levodopa (very low certainty):** not for standard use, only where short-term relief is valued over the risk of augmentation. [S1]
- ⚠️ **Do not use cabergoline** (strong recommendation against, moderate certainty). [S1]

## Drugs to avoid

- **Bupropion (moderate certainty), carbamazepine and valproic acid (low certainty), and clonazepam and valerian (very low certainty) are advised against** for RLS. [S1]

## Special groups

- **End-stage renal disease:** gabapentin (very low certainty), vitamin C (low certainty) and IV iron sucrose when ferritin is < 200 ng/mL and transferrin saturation < 20% (moderate certainty) are suggested. [S1]
- **End-stage renal disease:** levodopa and rotigotine are likewise not for standard use, for the same augmentation reason. [S1]
- **Children with RLS:** oral ferrous sulfate is suggested when iron status fits (very low certainty). [S1]
- **Periodic limb movement disorder in adults:** triazolam and valproic acid are advised against (very low certainty). [S1]
